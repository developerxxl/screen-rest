Screen Rest: Eye Break Timer

Smart 20-20-20 eye breaks and movement breaks for Mac, iPhone and iPad. Breaks wait for a natural pause and hold while your camera or microphone is in use, so they don't interrupt your calls.

The core break reminders are free. No account, no ads, no tracking.

This app supports healthy screen habits. It is not a medical device and does not diagnose or treat any condition. See an eye doctor if you have pain, sudden vision changes, flashes or floaters.
