Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 25, 2026

Screen Rest ("the app") is built to work on your device. There is no account, no advertising, no third-party analytics and no tracking.

Data that stays on your device

  • Your break history (when a break was due, taken, skipped or held) and daily active screen time on Mac.
  • Your settings, the optional first name you enter, and optional comfort check-ins.
  • On Mac, the app reads only how many seconds have passed since your last keyboard or mouse input, and whether a camera or microphone is in use. It never reads what you type, your screen contents or window titles, and it never records audio or video.
  • If you turn on calendar-aware breaks, the app reads only the start time, end time and busy status of your calendar events, on your device. It does not store or send event titles, people or notes.
  • On iPhone and iPad, if you allow Screen Time access for full-screen breaks, the app uses Apple's Screen Time frameworks only to show a rest screen at break time. It cannot see which apps you use, and nothing about it leaves your device.

Data that leaves your device

  • Purchases. Subscriptions are processed by Apple. We use RevenueCat to check whether your subscription is active. RevenueCat receives an anonymous app user ID and your purchase receipts. It does not receive your name or email.
  • Contact form. If you send a message from Contact us in Settings, the name, email address and message you type are sent by email to us through a form service (FormSubmit) so we can reply. Nothing else is sent.
  • Referral program. Only if you open the referral program, the app stores a random device identifier, your referral code, how many people used your code, and whether you earned a free month, in our database hosted by Supabase. Codes you enter are checked against this database.

What we never do

We never sell your data, show ads, or share your data with data brokers.

Deleting your data

In the app, open Settings and tap "Delete all history". Deleting the app removes all local data. To remove your referral record, contact us from the contact page.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
