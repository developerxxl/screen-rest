Breaks appear during my calls

On Mac, open Settings and make sure "Hold during calls" is on. Breaks wait while any app is using your camera or microphone.

I don't get reminders on my iPhone

Allow notifications for the app in iPhone Settings, and check that reminders are on and that your reminder hours include the current time.

How do I restore my Pro subscription?

Open Settings in the app and tap Restore Purchases.

How do I cancel my subscription?

On iPhone or iPad, go to Settings → your name → Subscriptions. On Mac, open the App Store → your account → Subscriptions.

How do I delete my data?

Open Settings in the app and tap "Delete all history". See our Privacy Policy.
